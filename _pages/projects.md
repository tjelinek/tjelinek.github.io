- [**OMa: 3D Object Reconstruction from Videos**](https://tjelinek.github.io/oma/) — model-free reconstruction of a rigid object from a single hand-held video, robust to a moving object and to a masked input.
- [**Dense Matchers for Dense Tracking**](/dense-tracking/) — fusing a wide-baseline dense matcher into MFT turns a strictly causal long-term point tracker into a competitor of non-causal sparse trackers.
- [**Open-Set 2D Detection: Template Condensation**](/template-condensation/) — condensing an onboarding-video template bank to a handful of diverse views per object, removing 90 to 99 percent of templates while keeping detection quality, without a CAD model.
- [**Differentiable Object Reconstruction and Tracking**](/differentiable-reconstruction/) — a differentiable-rendering approach to joint 6DoF pose and mesh recovery, and the two failure modes that ended it.
