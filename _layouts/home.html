I am a 3D perception engineer working on methods in 3D computer vision. My experience spans dense matching, 6DoF pose estimation, differentiable rendering, and object detection in both 2D images and 3D scenes.
Previously, I worked as a researcher in the Visual Recognition Group at the Czech Technical University in Prague. I received my B.Sc. and M.Sc. in Computer Science from Masaryk University.
